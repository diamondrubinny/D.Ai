Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · effective 19 October 2026

In short: this website collects nothing about you. It has no cookies and no trackers. D.Ai OS sends us nothing: its AI, dictation and search run on your computer. The only information that reaches us is what you choose to send, such as an e-mail you write to us.

1. Who we are

Diamond Rubinny (d.Ai), who develops and publishes D.Ai OS as an open-source project ("we"). We are the controller of the information described here. For any privacy question or request: privacy@rubinny.com.

2. What this policy covers

  • The website rubinny.com and all its pages ("the website").
  • The official releases of D.Ai OS, published on the project's GitHub Releases, and the official update service.

It does not cover other people's websites and services, even when the website or the system links to them. They have their own privacy policies.

3. The website

What we collect

Nothing. The website has no cookies, scripts, analytics, advertising pixels, forms or content from other sites (not even fonts). Its Content Security Policy blocks all of these from loading in the first place.

The hosting provider

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub's servers receive technical data to deliver and protect the website, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. GitHub processes this data under its privacy statement. We do not receive this data from GitHub and do not use it.

Cookies

The website uses no cookies, so it has no cookie banner. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first. Non-essential cookies would then load only after your explicit consent, and refusing or withdrawing consent would be just as easy as giving it.

4. Downloads and updates

  • Downloading the system: the installation files are downloaded from GitHub. GitHub sees the download's technical data (such as your IP address) under its privacy statement. We see only an overall download count, with nothing that identifies you.
  • Debian security updates: the computer contacts Debian's servers and mirrors. As with any Debian system, they see your IP address and the packages downloaded.
  • D.Ai updates come from a signed repository on GitHub Releases, only when you ask for them.
  • AI and dictation models are downloaded once, when you choose to download them, from Hugging Face. Each is checked against a fixed SHA-256 fingerprint. After that they run on the computer without an internet connection.
  • Clock: like any operating system, the system synchronizes its clock with public time servers.

5. The system on your computer

D.Ai OS sends no telemetry, usage data, crash reports or content of any kind to us or anyone else. The data D.Ai's programs keep stays on the computer, in the user's own folder, readable only by that user:

Questions to Diamond, voice dictation and file search are processed on the computer only.

When you choose to connect to others

  • Mail: J connects to the mail servers you set up. They receive what any mail program sends them.
  • The web: websites you visit in the Ruby browser see what any website sees. Ruby is set to block tracking, with Mozilla's telemetry and studies turned off. Add-ons and their updates come from Mozilla.
  • More software: when you install software from Flathub or another provider, its developer's privacy policy applies to it.
  • Reporting a problem: dai-raz report creates a text with your user name, computer name, IP addresses, e-mail addresses and personal paths removed. It is shown to you, and nothing is sent. If you choose to post it on GitHub Issues, it becomes public.

6. When you contact us

If you e-mail us, we use your address and message only to reply and to handle your request. We keep them until the request is handled and for up to 12 months after, then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. We do not use them for marketing and do not pass them to others unless the law requires it. Our e-mail provider processes the messages on our behalf.

Legal bases (GDPR, for visitors from the European Union):

  • Replying to you, and operating and securing the website: legitimate interests.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep: a legal obligation.

7. Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations, and under the GDPR where it applies to you, you may ask:

  • to see the information we hold about you, and to get a copy;
  • to correct it, delete it, or restrict its use;
  • to object to its use.

Write to privacy@rubinny.com, and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to Israel's Privacy Protection Authority or, in the European Union, to your country's data protection authority.

In practice, we hold almost nothing about you: the system's data is on your computer, where you can see, export and delete it yourself.

8. Transfers outside Israel

GitHub and Hugging Face are US companies. Technical data they process (for example during a download) may be transferred to the United States and other countries, under the safeguards described in their privacy statements.

9. Children

The website collects no information from anyone, children included. The education edition of D.Ai OS sends us nothing about pupils. The school that installs it is responsible for the data kept on its computers.

10. Security

The website is served over HTTPS only. Every release is published with SHA-256 checksums, and updates are signed. The system itself has disk encryption, a firewall, AppArmor and automatic security updates. To report a security issue, see the security policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with the date it takes effect. The full history of changes is public in the code repository on GitHub. If a change would allow collecting information not described here, we will ask for your consent first.
