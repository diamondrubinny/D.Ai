Accessibility statement

Updated 19 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website and D.Ai OS, including people with disabilities. Accessibility is part of how we build, not something added at the end.

1. The website

The level we aim for

The website is built to meet Israeli Standard 5568 and WCAG 2.1 at level AA. It has not yet been audited by a certified accessibility consultant. That audit is planned before we claim full conformance.

What we did

  • Semantic HTML, with a correct heading order and labelled regions.
  • A "skip to content" link, full keyboard navigation and a clear focus outline.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1, checked automatically on every change.
  • Detailed alternative text for every screenshot.
  • Tables with marked headers. Statuses are shown with a mark and words (✓, ≈, ✗), not by color alone.
  • No scripts, pop-ups, moving content or sound. Animations stop when "reduce motion" is on.
  • The website works at 200% zoom and on phones.
  • It is available in eight languages, including right-to-left Hebrew and Arabic.

Known limitations

  • Screenshots contain small text from the system. Their content is described in the alternative text.
  • On phones, the comparison table scrolls sideways, inside a labelled region you can reach with the keyboard.
  • The legal documents are available in Hebrew and English only.
  • Links to GitHub lead to a site we do not control.

2. The operating system

  • Every D.Ai window passes an automated accessibility check before each release: an accessible name for every control, keyboard navigation and contrast. The check runs in all three themes: dark, light and high contrast.
  • The system has a magnifier (Super+=), an on-screen keyboard (Super+Alt+K), night light, and a high-contrast theme ("high contrast" in D.Ai Tune).
  • Everything can also be done with a written or spoken sentence, without a mouse.

3. Found a problem? Tell us

If something on the website or in the system is not accessible to you, we want to know and fix it.

  • Accessibility coordinator: Diamond Rubinny
  • E-mail: accessibility@rubinny.com

Please say which page or window, what you were trying to do, and which browser or assistive technology you used. We will reply within 14 days, and let you know when it is fixed.
